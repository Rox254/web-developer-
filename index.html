Questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything Kenyan business owners ask us before getting started.

Is Ksh 10,000 really the total price?

Yes. Ksh 10,000 is the full, one-time cost for your website — design, development, domain setup and hosting included. There are no monthly fees, no yearly renewal charges and no hidden costs.

Do I have to pay for domain and hosting separately?

No — and this is exactly what makes us different. Most web designers build your site and then leave you to pay for a domain name and hosting every year, which can cost you Ksh 8,000–15,000 annually. With us, we host your website for you. It's all covered in your one-time Ksh 10,000.

How long does it take to get my website?

Most websites are designed, built and published within 3 working days after you send us your content (photos, text, contact details). Larger sites with online shops may take slightly longer.

What if I don't have a logo or photos?

No problem. We can design a professional logo for you (+ Ksh 3,000), and we can use clean stock imagery or arrange a photo session for your business (+ from Ksh 4,000).

How do I pay?

We accept M-Pesa. You pay 50% (Ksh 5,000) to start, and the remaining Ksh 5,000 once you've seen your website and you're happy with it. No payment is required until you approve the design.

Can I make changes after the website goes live?

Yes. You get free content updates for 12 months — new prices, new photos, new services, changed phone numbers. Just send a WhatsApp message and we'll handle it. After 12 months, updates are available at a small fee, or you can take our optional monthly plan.

Will my website work on phones?

Absolutely. Every website we build is designed mobile-first because the majority of Kenyan customers browse on their phones. Your site will look great on phones, tablets and computers.

Will customers find my business on Google?

We set up your page titles, descriptions, structure and Google Maps location so your business can appear in search results for your services and town. We also offer a Google Business Profile setup as an optional extra.

Do I own the website?

Yes — the website content, design and branding belong to you. We simply host and maintain it for you as part of your package.

Do you work with businesses outside Nairobi?

Yes. We work with businesses all over Kenya — Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Thika, Nyeri, Machakos and beyond. Everything is done through WhatsApp and email, so distance is never a problem.

What if I already have a website?

We can redesign it, move it to our hosting, or build you a brand new one. Send us the link on WhatsApp and we'll advise you on the best option.

Still have a question?

Ask us anything on WhatsApp. We reply fast and we don't do hard selling.
